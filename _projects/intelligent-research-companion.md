## Overview

The Intelligent Research Companion is a **multi-agent academic paper analysis system** designed to reduce the manual effort required for literature review and knowledge extraction.

The system combines **Mistral OCR**, citation extraction, automated reference retrieval, **LangChain**, **RAG**, and locally hosted LLMs through **Ollama**. Its hierarchical architecture includes an orchestrator, coordinator agents, and task-specific agents that decompose paper-analysis workflows into specialized steps.

The resulting workflow supports structured extraction of paper content, references, and research context, making it easier to build traceable literature-review pipelines.
